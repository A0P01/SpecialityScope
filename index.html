Is there a "surgeon personality"?

Less than TikTok says.

In a study of 2,345 board-certified physicians, surgeons scored a little higher on extraversion and openness and lower on neuroticism than medical specialists, with no difference in agreeableness or conscientiousness (Stienen et al., BMJ Open 2018). Those differences are small, and people overlap a lot. So SpecialtyScope matches you on what each field's daily work demands, like patient contact, emergencies, sad outcomes and hands-on work, instead of stereotypes about who "belongs" there.

Your application

Estimated chance to match

—

How this works: start from the share of U.S. seniors who matched when this specialty was their first choice (NRMP 2026). For MD seniors, we compare your Step 2 CK with the scores of matched and unmatched applicants. Then we nudge for your profile. NRMP does not publish exact effects for research, letters, AOA or rotations, so those nudges are our rough assumptions.

Your numbers

Accepted to at least one MD school

—

This is the share of past applicants with your GPA and MCAT range who were accepted to at least one U.S. MD school (AAMC Table A-23, three application cycles combined: 2023–24 through 2025–26, 155,940 applicants). It is not a prediction for any single school, and it leaves out DO schools, clinical hours, research, essays and interviews.
